# Character concept — illustration prompt (v1)

Paste into an image generator (the tool used for the original character sheet, Midjourney, ChatGPT/DALL·E, Ideogram, Firefly, Gamma…).
Ask for a **16:9** image. Save the result in this folder as `concept-v1.png` (PNG, as large as the tool allows).

```
Character concept sheet for an animated social-media promo. ONE original character shown twice, identical face, hair and
outfit in both views: on the LEFT a natural full-body standing pose, on the RIGHT an expressive waist-up view talking to the viewer.

Character: a young Ghanaian man in his early 20s, a relatable university-age storyteller. Warm medium-dark brown skin. Neat low
fade haircut with short textured coils on top and a sharp hairline. Thick expressive eyebrows, bright friendly eyes, refined
natural facial anatomy with a defined jaw, a light hint of a goatee. Slim, natural young-adult proportions (about 7.5 heads tall),
not childish, not exaggerated.

Outfit: contemporary smart-casual streetwear. An open deep-navy overshirt jacket over a warm cream t-shirt, relaxed tapered tan
chinos, clean white sneakers, a slim dark smartwatch. Convincing fabric folds at the elbows, waist and knees.

Full-body pose (left): relaxed, slightly asymmetrical contrapposto stance, weight on one leg, one hand casually in his trouser
pocket, the other hand raised in an open, well-drawn "so tell me" gesture, head tilted slightly, confident warm half-smile,
slight three-quarter angle.

Waist-up view (right): leaning in a little toward the viewer, one eyebrow raised, amused skeptical expression as if saying
"Chale, why is it always about money?", one hand open palm-up with clearly drawn fingers, shoulders relaxed and uneven.

Style: polished modern commercial editorial 2D illustration, the kind used in premium African fintech and startup advertising.
Clean confident shapes with thin dark contour lines, sophisticated layered cel shading with subtle soft gradients, warm rim
light, tasteful highlights on skin, expressive well-constructed hands, distinctive silhouette. Flat 2D illustration, NOT 3D
render, not plastic, not photorealistic, not childish cartoon.

Background: plain soft pale lavender-white studio background with a subtle gradient, no scenery. No text, no logos, no labels,
no watermark, no extra characters.
```

Outfit colours are deliberately navy / cream / tan so he stands apart from Dexterity-orange graphics and backgrounds.
