Notes
- `8.09.04 PM.mp4` and `8.11.16 PM.mp4` decode to **identical samples** (same take sent twice); only one is used.
- All six "WhatsApp Audio" files are a male voice (≈100–115 Hz) — including the three narrator takes. Their pitch matches Kofi's, so the
  narrator is very likely the same performer as Kofi. Say so if you want a different narrator.
- The two "Ptt" (push-to-talk) files are the female voice (≈200–235 Hz) = Ama.
- No dialogue was synthesised or cloned. The old Kokoro TTS files in `public/audio/s*_*.mp3` are no longer used.
- Phrase timings (used for subtitle sync and music ducking) were measured from the audio envelope; word timing *inside* a phrase is estimated by character weight.
- Scene 4: one 1.0 s pause between "check them out" and "You'll be happy you did" was shortened to 0.45 s (a splice, not a speed change).
